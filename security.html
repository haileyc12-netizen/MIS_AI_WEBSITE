Malware and Cyber Threats

Colonial Pipeline Ransomware Attack (2021)

Colonial Pipeline was hit by ransomware on May 7, 2021. The attack was done by an online criminal group called Darkside. Because the ransomware messed up the company’s computers, it had to temporarily stop running its pipelines that moved fuel along the East Coast of the United States. Because of the shutdown, there wasn’t enough fuel, people bought out a lot of it quickly, and gas prices went up in some places. It is said that Colonial Pipeline paid a ransom of about $4.4 million to get back into its systems. This event was significant because it showed how vulnerable critical infrastructure is to cyberattacks and how important it is to have strong cybersecurity protections.
